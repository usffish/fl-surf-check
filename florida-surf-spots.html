Marker size and shade both carry the same value, so the ranking survives greyscale and colour-blindness. The Keys are drawn but hold no spots — they face the wrong way and sit behind the reef.

This map is specific to one origin, 33613 — the last one was not. Everything here comes from the same formula the CLI runs: for every daylight hour in the record, swell rarity (log-space standard deviations above one pooled statewide baseline built from the full record — not a moving seasonal window; that was removed on request, since a normal August day and a normal February day are now judged against the same distribution) plus a wind adjustment, maxed over each day, minus that spot's real OSRM drive time from Tampa at 120 min/σ. The vectorised version was checked against the live scoring.py functions — using the actual cached baseline, not a stand-in — and matched to six decimal places before this ran across 41 spots × ~1,800 days each.

What changes once distance is priced in

The best surf in the state nearly disappears. Jupiter Inlet has the best swell climate in Florida on the pooled record. Priced from Tampa at four hours each way, it clears the value bar on just 4.6% of days. Distance did almost all of that work.

Apollo Beach takes over at the top — 18.8%. Not because it is the best water (it isn't; Jupiter's is meaningfully better), but because the combination of decent swell and a ~3-hour drive clears 120 min/σ more often than anywhere further south does.

The Gulf spots hold their own on value even though their surf is weak. Honeymoon Island (8.2%) and Clearwater (7.0%) rank ahead of Cocoa Beach, Fort Pierce, Sebastian Inlet and every Palm Beach and Broward spot — all better surf, most of them three-plus hours further away. A 55-minute drive needs far less swell to clear the bar than a three-hour one, and the map shows it winning that trade repeatedly.

South Florida and the Panhandle both go dark. Miami-Dade, Broward and the Panhandle spots round out the bottom — not because the surf is always bad (Palm Beach and Boca Raton are respectable water) but because 4.5–8.5 hours is a drive the value score essentially never clears at this rate.
